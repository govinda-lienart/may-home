Where we are

The perfect base camp

We sit at a rare intersection — one footstep from the sea, yet close to two major cities. Everything is within reach, but you'll always have peace to come back to.

🏖

Ha My Beach

1 min walk

Step outside and you're already there. A quiet, uncrowded stretch of sand — your private morning beach.

🌊

An Bang Beach

8 min by motorbike

One of Vietnam's most beloved beaches — beach bars, sunbeds, fresh seafood, and clear water.

🏮

Hoi An Old Town

10 min by motorbike

Lanterns, ancient streets, tailor shops, and the best bowl of cao lầu you'll ever taste.

🌆

Da Nang City

10 min by motorbike

International airport, Dragon Bridge, city restaurants, and everything you might need.

Things to Explore

You're in one of Vietnam's most breathtaking regions. The Central Coast has ancient history, dramatic mountains, pristine beaches, and world-class food — all within a short ride.

The Central Coast

Marble Mountains, My Son Sanctuary, Hai Van Pass, the Cham Islands — endless adventures from your front door.

🛵

One of the world's great coastal drives. 45 min away.

⛵
Cham Islands

Snorkelling, diving, and pristine beaches. Day trip by boat.
